NutriAI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

NutriAI does not collect your data. Not for analytics, not for advertising, not for “improving the service”. There are no accounts and no servers belonging to us, so there is nowhere for your information to be sent even if we wanted it.

This policy describes what that means precisely.

What stays on your device

Everything you put into NutriAI is stored only on your phone:

  • your profile, goals and targets
  • your food diary, custom foods, recipes and meal plans
  • weight, measurements, exercise, water, fasting and cycle entries
  • lab and blood-marker entries
  • saved restaurant items and meals, and your dietary and allergen choices
  • app settings and preferences

This information is held in your device’s own storage. It is encrypted at rest, with the key held in the iOS Keychain. We cannot read it, and we cannot recover it for you — if you delete the app without making a backup, it is gone.

Backups are yours

You can export an encrypted backup. The archive is encrypted with a passphrase you choose, and it goes wherever you send it (Files, AirDrop, your own cloud storage). We never receive it. If you lose the passphrase, nobody — including us — can open the file.

The only network request the app makes

NutriAI contacts exactly one service: Open Food Facts, the open food database, and only to look up food you asked about.

  • Scanning a barcode sends the barcode digits.
  • Searching food sends the text you typed in the search box.
  • If a product has a photo, the app may fetch that photo.

Nothing else is ever sent — not your name, not your diary, not your goals, not an identifier for you or your device. There is no account, no login and no tracking. Open Food Facts receives these requests as an anonymous visitor, subject to their own privacy policy.

You can switch this off entirely. With online lookup disabled, the app makes no network requests at all.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, NutriAI reads the data types you approve and may write entries you create. This exchange happens on your device, between NutriAI and Apple Health. Nothing is transmitted to us. You control the permissions in the Health app, and can revoke them at any time.

Camera, microphone and photos

  • Camera is used to scan barcodes, on your device.
  • Photos are used to read a nutrition label or menu you choose, on your device.
  • Microphone and speech recognition are used for voice logging. Speech recognition may be processed by Apple according to Apple’s privacy policy; NutriAI does not store or transmit recordings.

None of these produce data sent to us.

What we do not do

  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, share or disclose your information, because we do not have it.

Children

NutriAI is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Health information is educational only

NutriAI provides nutrition information and general guidance. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it is not a substitute for advice from a qualified health professional.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date. Because the app holds your data locally, a change to this policy cannot retroactively affect information we never received.
