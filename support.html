NutriAI stores everything on your device, so please include what you were doing when something went wrong — there are no server logs for us to look at.

Where is my data?

On your phone, encrypted, and nowhere else. There is no account and no server. If you delete the app without exporting a backup, the data is gone and cannot be recovered — not by you, and not by us.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

Open Privacy & data, then Back up now. You choose a passphrase and the encrypted file goes wherever you send it. Restore it on the new device with the same passphrase. Keep that passphrase safe: nobody can open the file without it.

A nutrition number looks wrong

Every restaurant figure is the chain’s own published information for your country, with the source and retrieval date shown in the app. Open that source and compare. If the chain has changed a figure, a later app update brings the new data with a new retrieval date.

Why does the app need the camera or microphone?

The camera scans barcodes, photos read a nutrition label you choose, and the microphone is for voice logging. All optional, and none of it is sent to us.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The only network request is looking up a barcode or a food search you typed, and you can turn that off entirely.
